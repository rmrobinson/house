Policy engine not connected

This page needs a running policyd instance, but adminui isn't configured to reach one.

To enable it, set adminui.policy_addr in your adminui.yaml to that policyd's PolicyService address, then restart adminui:

adminui:
  policy_addr: "192.168.1.5:1338"

See adminui.example.yaml for the full config shape. The Policies and Execution Log links stay dimmed in the nav until this is set.
